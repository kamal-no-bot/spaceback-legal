Common questions

I cleared things and my storage did not change

That is iOS, not the app, and it is the most important thing to know about cleaning a phone. Deleted photos and videos go to Recently Deleted and sit there for thirty days before the space comes back. Until you empty that album yourself, your phone is still holding them.

Disk Buddy is honest about it: the receipt prints two separate figures, what was sent to Recently Deleted and what your phone actually returned, read before and after. No app can empty Recently Deleted for you.

Why is the second figure on the receipt smaller than the first?

Because of the album above, and because some of what you cleared may have lived in iCloud rather than on the phone, so removing it freed nothing locally. The first figure is what left your library. The second is what the phone gave back. Both are true and the app prints both rather than the flattering one.

The app found nothing

Then there is nothing of that kind to find, and the app says so rather than inventing something. The first screen still works without a single duplicate: it prints what your phone holds, what is free, and how long the room lasts at the rate you have been filling it.

How do I get my money back or cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by the app. Open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. The Drawer has a Manage subscription row that takes you straight there.

I bought the Pass and it is not on

Open the Drawer and tap Find what I bought. That asks the App Store again for anything this Apple Account owns. If a purchase is waiting on a parent's approval, the app says so on the purchase screen and turns the Pass on by itself when the approval comes through.

Can I change the look?

Yes. The Drawer has Paper and mark: a white sheet or a warm till roll. Picking one changes the paper the app prints on and the icon on your home screen together, so the app and its icon always match.

Where are the contact backups?

In the Files app. The Drawer has a Backups row that opens the folder holding them, so you can read one, copy it off the phone, or import it back into Contacts if a merge was not what you wanted.

What does the app never do?

No weekly plan. No adverts, ever. No notification telling you your phone is nearly full. No claims about cache, memory, battery or speed, because iOS allows an app none of them. No contact merged before a backup is written. All of that is printed inside the app, on the refusals card in the Drawer.
